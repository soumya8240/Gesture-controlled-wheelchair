# Gesture-controlled-wheelchair
i had made a gesture controlled wheelchair , it is designed for the patients who are effected with paralysis from the waist down. Commercially available joystick based wheelchair and expensive and it requires proper fingure coordination to control the wheelchair. This prototype can be controlled with just wrist gestures/tilt.This wheelchair also contains a safety function ; if the wheelchair gets tuppled over , then a buzzer will go on and the wheels will not respond untill the chair is made upright
This project contains two parts transmitter module and reciever module.
The transmitter  module is to be worn on the wrist. When the patient tilt his hands forward , backward , right and left the wheelchair moves accordingly.
I have used Arduino Uno , MPU6050, Tilt sensor , piezo buzzer , HC-05 Bluetooth module , Arduino nano 
This is just a small prototype that can be made with approx Rs 1000.
This project can be scaled to a much extend. We can also integrate AI model in it for more personalised and precise controll and safety
